# **Code of Conduct**

## **Our Commitment**

We want this project to be a welcoming and inclusive space for everyone.
All contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to act respectfully and professionally.

## **Expected Behavior**

* Be kind, patient, and respectful to others.
* Provide constructive feedback, not personal attacks.
* Assume good intentions and help newcomers.
* Collaborate openly — share knowledge and improvements.
* Follow project guidelines and maintain technical quality.

## **Unacceptable Behavior**

The following will **not** be tolerated:

* Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language
* Trolling, insults, or personal threats
* Sharing private information without permission
* Posting malicious code, exploits, or harmful content
* Disrupting project activities or spamming

## **Community Standards**

* Treat everyone with dignity and professionalism.
* Welcome diverse opinions, but disagree respectfully.
* Keep discussions focused on the project and its goals.
* Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

## **Reporting Issues**

If you observe a violation of this Code of Conduct:

* Report it privately to the project maintainers.
* Provide any relevant info so we can investigate.

All reports will be handled confidentially.

## **Enforcement**

Project maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

* Warning the offender
* Removing inappropriate content
* Temporarily or permanently banning contributors

Decisions made by maintainers are final.

## **Acknowledgment**

This Code of Conduct is inspired by open-source community values and lightweight versions of the Contributor Covenant.
